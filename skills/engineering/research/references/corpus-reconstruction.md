# Corpus research for visual and game reconstruction

## 1. Ground the corpus and existing store

Read the project research contract, source manifest, existing subject research, database schema, importers and reconstruction queries. Verify the source fingerprint before trusting old citations. Inspect the artifact's component title pages and end boundaries; record included versions, pre-release text and absent works instead of inferring coverage from a collection filename. Audit appended comprehensive glossaries and other editorial supplements independently: a verified artifact fingerprint does not authenticate every component's authorship. Keep unidentified compilations as qualified leads rather than inheriting the neighboring novel's evidence tier; replace their support with verified narrative passages where possible and preserve any corrective disposition.

For archived interviews and signing reports, follow the archive's exact original-publication links with redirects before constructing replacement URLs; a failed guessed URL does not establish that the original is unavailable. Read the original question, answer and reporting context. Recovering an attendee's contemporaneous post verifies what that reporter published, not necessarily a verbatim author statement or its applicability to an opening-story state. Preserve paraphrase, recording uncertainty, event versus posting dates and unknown story timing. Compare publication bylines and body dates with mastheads, archive labels and URL dates rather than silently choosing one.

Capture repository and database baselines and use SQLite's backup API for a recoverable database snapshot. Open investigative connections with `sqlite3.connect(path.resolve().as_uri() + '?mode=ro', uri=True)`. Perform research and candidate preparation away from the active library; original documents remain provenance, not a second independently edited facts store.

## 2. Extract source-native locators and build the search matrix

### EPUB identity and locator preflight

1. Fingerprint every candidate file, then use standard-library `zipfile` and `xml.etree.ElementTree` to inspect `META-INF/container.xml`, its OPF package, metadata, manifest and spine. Preserve exact filenames and identifiers. Compare OPF credits with actual title/copyright pages: package metadata can omit coauthors. Record proofing/conversion notices and edition uncertainty instead of treating a clean extraction as an authenticated edition.
2. Check NCX `pageList`, navigation elements with `epub:type="page-list"`, OPF page-map declarations and `epub:type="pagebreak"` markers before promising numerical citations. Count spine documents as documents, not pages; reader screen numbers, paragraph offsets and text percentages are not artifact page numbers.
3. If no embedded pagination is found, report that bounded result rather than declaring EPUBs uncitable. Check whether the project's citation contract permits work/chapter plus an internal XHTML URI and an existing fragment, reproducible passage index or verified CFI, accompanied by a short exact quote. If the contract requires page numbers, obtain the required citation-policy approval before using another locator; do not invent pagination to pass the gate.
4. Inspect strict XML failures before rejecting the narrative. For ordinary HTML named entities such as `&rsquo;` or `&ldquo;`, test standard-library `html.parser.HTMLParser(convert_charrefs=True)` against the actual document; it can recover readable text without altering the EPUB. Preserve inline text and Unicode punctuation; do not silently repair OCR spellings.
5. Extract visible quotation, list and definition containers as well as paragraphs and headings; otherwise a paragraph-only reader can silently omit glossaries, epigraphs or direct quotations. Emit nested containers once. Compare captured text with normalized visible body text and inspect discrepancies; nonempty output or a character-count threshold is a gap detector, not proof of complete capture. Test inline formatting, entities, nested blockquotes, empty blocks and reproducible passage numbering before distributing extracts.
6. Resolve logical work, section and chapter membership in spine order from actual headings and boundary context. Carry a chapter through document splits only until a verified boundary; a new XHTML file is not necessarily a new chapter, and an appended preview is not part of the preceding glossary. Combine separate NCX number/title labels targeting the same boundary. When navigation only says “Start” or conflicts with the text, retain an explicit source-unit map with actual work/section, eligibility and boundary evidence. Keep original locators and raw navigation labels unchanged, and test that excluded or other-work units cannot support accepted target-work facts.
7. Inspect schema, retrieval and validator assumptions before publication. Never put an EPUB passage index into `pdf_page`, use a dummy page to satisfy a guard, or convert a legacy PDF citation by percentage. Preserve the old artifact identity and evidence; add or reconcile separately verified support from the new artifact.

### Inspect embedded visual source units

1. Inventory image-only documents and packaged image assets as well as captured text. Resolve XHTML, SVG and CSS references and compare them with the archive's asset list, because a paragraph extractor cannot establish visual coverage or reveal an unreferenced alternative.
2. Make labeled contact sheets for broad classification, then inspect relevant maps or embedded text at native resolution. Treat covers and symbolic chapter artwork as illustrations, not literal character anatomy or environmental descriptions.
3. Diagnose an apparently blank asset from the original archive bytes before replacing the source: check the referencing markup, unused packaged assets, native pixels/alpha and contrast. Where Pillow is available, `Image.open(...).crop(box).getextrema()` distinguishes a uniformly blank region from faint recoverable detail; a thumbnail or coarse nonwhite threshold alone does not. Preserve asset paths, hashes and inspected dispositions.
4. If required visual information is absent, record a source-quality gap and stop dependent conclusions; continue independent prose research. Do not silently replace the approved artifact or call a text-only result complete visual-source coverage. Obtain the applicable source/scope decision once.

### PDF page-preserving extraction

Reuse installed Poppler and standard-library Python where sufficient:

```python
result = subprocess.run(
    ['pdftotext', '-enc', 'UTF-8', str(pdf), '-'],
    capture_output=True, check=True,
)
pages = result.stdout.decode('utf-8').split('\f')
assert not pages[-1].strip(), 'Missing terminal page delimiter'
pages.pop()
```

Remove the terminal empty split before counting physical pages; a final form feed otherwise creates a fictitious extra page. Check actual page count, component boundaries and nonempty relevant pages before reporting the inventory, including inventories supplied by reviewers. Assign physical pages with `enumerate(pages, 1)`, not corpus line counts. Keep full extracted copyright text in research scratch, not in project deliverables. Specify `-enc UTF-8` explicitly: Windows Poppler defaults can produce non-UTF-8 bytes. Pass the stated scratch directory as an explicit native Windows path when shell and Python `TMPDIR` disagree; do not let a shell fallback put extraction in system temp.

For multi-column papers, inspect the relevant physical page's layout before joining extracted sentence fragments. When PyMuPDF is available, use `page.get_text('blocks')`: each block provides its bounding box in `block[:4]` and text in `block[4]`. Confirm the continuation across the column boundary and record both the printed page and physical PDF page; render the page if the block order is ambiguous. Plain full-page extraction order is not proof of paragraph order.

Define executable case-insensitive term groups for names, historical names, extraction variants, landmarks, neighboring geography, roads, residents, trades, construction, festivals and relevant activities. Audit extracted tokens sharing distinctive name stems and search spaced/hyphenated forms before freezing the matrix; exact-name searches can miss split words even when the surrounding text extracts correctly. Inspect the candidates rather than adding every prefix match as an alias. Search every group across every included component, with zero-hit cells explicit. Persist each hit's stable ID, work, group, source-native passage locator (PDF page/offset or EPUB document/block), and inspected context, plus each cell's exact regex, range, count and actual search time. Do not rewrite imported historical counts as fresh searches.

## 3. Read contexts efficiently without skipping them

Group candidate windows by source document/page, sort **all** intervals by block or character offset, then merge overlaps. Query-group iteration does not produce reading order; merging unsorted intervals can omit context. Count unique review windows separately from overlapping group hits when assigning work. Read exports in medium, wrapped batches so long single lines do not silently hide text in truncated tool output. Expand into neighboring passages until speaker, location, scene and qualification are resolved; stop the source-window grouping from becoming a chapter-boundary assumption.

Classify every hit as useful, duplicate or false positive with an inspected-context rationale. Unrelated character mentions, color/Ajah uses of Green, another town's forge and publisher boilerplate are not village facts. Do not classify unread hits automatically from keywords or apply one blanket disposition to a whole book. Duplicate means repeated evidence for the same scoped assertion, not merely the same word.

Build finite review packets containing exact target keys/hit IDs and deduplicated, source-ordered context. Pilot one representative packet before scaling; size later packets from verified review completion, not a fixed book or hit count. After actually reading a target block and resolving its context, expand its reasoned review to overlapping term-group hits for that same block; this avoids repeated reading without mechanically classifying unseen text. Require one review per target and exactly one disposition per assigned hit, and validate finding links separately.

Persist a continuation manifest with assigned, completed and unresolved IDs plus input/output artifact paths, so a partial worker return resumes the remainder rather than restarting the campaign or disappearing behind a completion message. Count only useful/duplicate/false-positive dispositions; a ledger can contain every ID while leaving most rows unresolved. Preserve worker originals and validate exact ID sets at ingestion.

Before dispatch, combine concrete names, spelling variants and service/detail terms from existing research into the initial matrix; collecting them only after broad reviews finish creates avoidable follow-up passes. For each returned lead, record its literal term/regex, source basis, actual referent and matching existing pattern or genuine gap. Keep already-covered terms and unrelated referents as explicit coverage decisions, not new subject aliases. Preserve artifact spellings as search variants without silently correcting the source or declaring new entities.

Batch compatible new gaps through the existing scanner across **every** included work in a separately identified expansion, retaining previous hit IDs and dispositions. Search distinctive names and landmarks directly; when generic detail words need a local-context filter, persist the exact executable filter and its bounded scope rather than claiming global word coverage. New cells reopen the affected coverage and final acceptance, not every previously verified disposition. Keep in-flight reviews bound to their original input/matrix version and carry the new pending delta forward; an empty earlier queue does not establish current completeness.

Reuse a previous inspected disposition only when source fingerprint, extractor/locator rules, passage location, match text and inspected context agree; retain the original hit link and disposition. If extraction or block numbering changes, preserve the previous run and regenerate/relink affected locators and hit IDs before accepting worker returns. Read changed or wider windows as new contexts. Exact-hit reuse is not itself a semantic “duplicate” disposition. Reconcile IDs and counts programmatically after the final term change, including zero cells. Explicitly distinguish exhaustive resolution of the declared matrix from a claim that no descriptive passage exists outside it.

## 4. Turn descriptions into reconstruction records

Collect atomic paraphrased claims with short exact evidence anchors, work/chapter and source-native passage locator, viewpoint or speaker, observation context, applicability basis and limits. Check support for every substantive clause: a short quote locating one feature does not substantiate all materials, counts and activities bundled into its paraphrase. Split the claim or add separately located fragments. Split observed architecture from neighboring hearsay.

Assign observation type per evidence fragment after reading the scene; a waking conversation can contain a memory, report or imagined comparison. Keep this type separate from the scene's setting and the described fact's validity time. Use explicit reviewed fields, not substring heuristics over prose, to choose dream/memory/dialogue classifications or temporal anchors.

For character/creature reference work:

1. Inventory the named scene's visible silhouette, face exposure, garment extent, surface response, pose, equipment and mount before assembling a general anatomy sheet. Keep bodily traits, temporary costume and viewpoint visibility separate; a later close-up can establish anatomy without establishing that it was exposed earlier.
2. Trace every adjective to its referent. A black covered silhouette does not establish black skin, a mounted figure does not establish the mount's breed, and a comparison to a snarling face does not establish a visible snarl. Store felt menace as a viewpoint reaction rather than invented facial geometry. Do not identify a beak's species from the feet, assign a pair's “one ... the other” features by list order alone, or turn “identity not established” into “definitely different individuals.” Keep later unidentified bodies as context rather than corroboration of a specific earlier individual.
3. Separate disappearance from its mechanism. When observation is interrupted and the subject is gone on the next look, record that sequence without depicting an unwitnessed portal, transformation or travel effect. Likewise distinguish a garment's response to wind from its movement with the wearer or its behavior after death.
4. Build the ordinary baseline from evidence explicitly concerning ordinary members; retain unusually large individuals, altered bodies, supernatural vessels and folklore exaggerations as separate qualified records. When a direct author answer links an earlier encounter to a later figure as an early version, retain that qualified relation without merging encounter bodies or transferring later stature/equipment. Compare the same feature, referent and state before declaring a contradiction: lean and muscular can coexist. Check garment parts and idiomatic plurals against explicit anatomy and action before proposing a continuity error; paired sleeves or cuffs do not establish paired limbs. Do not turn an exception into the species model or assign an unnamed encounter a later individual's identity without evidence.
5. Make two clearly labeled handoff sections when both are useful: the literal scene reconstruction and the general anatomical/costume reference. Cite the constraints and list concealed or unestablished features beside them; keep any proposed reveal, tailoring, material, scale or effect choice outside canon claims. Keep relative measurements relative when the comparator has no established absolute size. Do not convert a coarse color, build or size descriptor into a breed, genetic coat category, precise dimensions or unmentioned markings.
6. For a varied creature population, assign each source-described specimen a scene-anchored identity and collect its linked atomic features before building the cross-specimen feature matrix. Use columns for silhouette/scale, head/face, eyes/ears, coverings, hands, legs/feet, clothing/armor, equipment, markings/injuries and visibility limits. Cite each populated cell; distinguish explicitly absent features from unmentioned ones. Preserve multi-animal combinations on the same specimen, and treat lists describing a crowd as population alternatives rather than one composite body. Keep tribal insignia and other affiliations separate from anatomical classification.

### Character development across an image sequence

1. Establish the intended research timeline and image goal before splitting work. Retain a coverage owner for every work, but dispatch bounded passage batches under one shared findings/hit contract rather than letting each reviewer invent a final character design. Keep direct-author research and database reconciliation as separate assignments when independent.
2. For every candidate state record the event anchor, work/chapter and source-native locator; source-observed appearance and physical condition; clothing, equipment and marks; expression, posture and movement; reactions from other characters; viewpoint, observation context and uncertainties. Attach atomic claim/evidence candidates and existing-ID matches. Record coverage and zero-hit dimensions rather than supplying genre defaults.
3. Separate stable identity from changing presentation. A face described as harder or less open does not establish a new jaw shape, heavier brow or different bone structure. Treat those anatomical choices as proposals unless separately supported; preserve the possibility that the change is expression, tension, condition or another observer's impression.
4. Track authority independently from distress and emotional closure. Collect evidence of composure, command, hesitation, warmth, fear and others' reactions without assuming they rise or fall together. Preserve reversals, recovery and different social contexts; do not pick the coldest depiction as the inevitable endpoint.
5. Compile all in-scope evidence before selecting panel boundaries. Separate enduring changes from an injury, exhausting journey, disguise, dream or singular emotional crisis. Place panels at supported transitions; use multiple states within a work or combine unchanged intervals only where the evidence justifies it. Keep chronological development distinct from how old the character should physically appear.
6. Publish reconciled knowledge first, then derive a concise state-by-state image brief with claim/evidence IDs and unknowns. Keep camera, lighting, comparable framing and identity-consistency instructions in the external art-direction/prompt artifact, not knowledge claims. Acceptance should check both that later marks/equipment do not leak backward and that the selected states portray changes in presence rather than merely different costumes.

For building-material reconstruction, resolve the construction stack before producing a texture or shader brief:

| Description | What it supports | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Framed houses | Framed construction | Exposed half-timber styling, a particular infill or exterior cladding |
| Whitewashed walls | A described surface finish | Plaster beneath it, stone beneath it, or that every house shares the finish |
| Stone foundations | Foundation material | Stone wall infill, exposed plinth height or a masonry pattern |
| A nearby farmhouse's wooden walls | That building's wall fabric | A universal village construction rule |
| Later stone houses | Their qualified later observation | Opening construction without an earlier-applicability basis |

1. Query existing material claims, qualifiers and approvals before expanding the search. Verify short passages and their actual building referents on physical pages; preserve the distinction between immediate aftermath, rebuilding and later growth.
2. Trace the current model's choice through the controlling brief, generated prompt and approval record. A generator instruction can explain why plaster appeared without providing evidence that plaster is canon.
3. State the narrow gap: unspecified infill is not proof of all-stone walls, and absence of an explicit plaster term is not a ban on plaster as a proposed interpretation. Do not restart an exhaustive corpus campaign merely to answer a focused production question.
4. If the source remains underspecified, recommend one plausible construction stack using the closest applicable evidence, label its extrapolations, and request the creative decision. Record approval in the existing project decision/asset record rather than silently rewriting a canon claim. Keep recipe approval separate from acceptance of the resulting artwork.

Cover terrain and approaches; water and crossings; landmark directions and adjacency; building form, materials, roof/chimneys/shutters; interiors and service areas; vegetation and animals; trades and household props; ordinary and festival activity; sound, smell, season and lighting; damage, repair, defenses and later growth. Store unknown dimensions and shop coordinates explicitly. Do not substitute a plausible surveyed layout for relation evidence.

Verify chapter membership at the **raw-page quotation offset**, starting with the carried-over chapter and applying only headers preceding the quote. Whitespace-normalized search offsets are not raw-page offsets: locate the evidence quote back in raw text before comparing header positions. Keep work-local chapter boundaries separate from global story chronology.

At semantic acceptance, trace pronouns and speakers across paragraph/scene boundaries; distinguish the current town from a remembered home, and a historical battle site from a destroyed capital mentioned nearby. Resolve idioms and activity referents before turning them into props or geography: spending money is not displaying wares, watching bowls is not playing a tabletop game, and a forest-exit distance is not a farmland boundary. Check event sequence against the actual narrative instead of copying a draft's before/after label. Trace dream entry, interruptions and exit: an intrusive personal fantasy inside a dream-world visit is not a stable reflected environment. Keep corrections in the parent's normalized candidate without rewriting the researcher's original return.

Preserve dreams, visions, recollections, dialogue and reports as qualified observations. Later material can refine an opening reconstruction only with an explicit earlier-applicability basis. Depicted construction, proposed construction and completed construction are different states. Sudden dream weather and absent dream inhabitants do not establish waking climate or abandonment. Conflicting accounts remain linked; do not invent remodeling to reconcile them.

Confirm the normal scene query can retrieve new facts with internal context; inserting rows alone does not prove reconstruction usability.

Resolve every new quote against its verified PDF page or approved EPUB passage and local chapter boundary. Verify that every final work/term cell exists, every hit ID has exactly one disposition, useful/duplicate/false-positive totals equal raw counts, and persisted coverage equals the final scanner output. Run database integrity, foreign-key, temporal-range, orphan/provenance and duplicate checks. Exercise opening and changed-state queries with negative checks excluding later buildings, defenses and unqualified dream weather from the opening applicable set.
